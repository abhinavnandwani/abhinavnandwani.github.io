Abhinav Nandwani

Applied AI Labs @ AMD · B.S. Computer Engineering & Computer Science, UW–Madison

I work in Applied AI Labs at AMD on agentic AI for silicon design. I’ve helped bootstrap internal benchmarking of coding agents on real chip-design tasks—spec-to-RTL, waveform debug, and verification—using verifiable rewards from UVM testbenches and formal equivalence checks so measurements feed real improvement loops. I also built agentic waveform debug tooling (including a waveform MCP) used broadly across design teams, and I contribute to evaluating external AI vendors and startups building tools for chip design.

Before AMD I was an undergraduate researcher in Professor Daifeng Wang’s lab at UW–Madison, building inference and fine-tuning pipelines for single-cell genomics foundation models at PsychAD scale—distributed training on NVIDIA H200 clusters with PyTorch DDP and PEFT (LoRA/QLoRA). I published VRSight (CHI 2025) on AI-driven VR accessibility.

These days I’m especially interested in ML systems: reliable training and deployment, tooling around agents and hardware-aware ML, and where accelerators, compilers, and model stacks meet.
