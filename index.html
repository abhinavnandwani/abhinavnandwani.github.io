Abhinav Nandwani

Applied AI Labs @ AMD · Computer Engineering & Computer Science, UW–Madison

I work in Applied AI Labs at AMD on problems where large-scale machine learning meets real hardware: training and inference stacks, GPU throughput, and the systems around them. My background is computer engineering and computer science from UW–Madison, with roots in RTL, microarchitecture, and how silicon constraints show up in software.

These days I care most about ML systems—post-training and RL-style loops at scale, cloud and on-prem economics, and bridging what accelerators can do with what frameworks and operators actually need. I still draw on architecture-level thinking: where time and memory go, what dominates wall clock, and how to reason about that end to end.

On the research side, recent publications include work on AI-driven accessibility in VR (VRSight, CHI 2025). For a longer systems note, see the blog on RLVR training costs and GPU pricing.

Recent work

  • RLVR GPU training costs, benchmarks, and pricing — measured GRPO baselines, cloud list prices, citations, and an interactive cost estimator (2026).
  • VRSight — AI-driven scene descriptions for VR accessibility; full paper and CHI demo (Google Scholar).
